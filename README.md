# Pinch & Pan

**Turn the ingredients you already have into realistic meal options that fit your health goals, time, and cooking level.**

## Live Demo

👉 https://pinch-and-pan-recipe-picker.replit.app

## What It Does

Pinch & Pan helps users decide what to cook based on the ingredients they already have.

Users can:

- Select ingredients they currently have
- Choose health and cooking preferences
- Get realistic meal recommendations
- See calorie estimates and health tags
- See which ingredients are missing
- Open a YouTube tutorial for the meal

## Why We Built It

Many people already have food at home but still order takeout because deciding what to cook takes time and effort.

Our goal is simple:

**Cook more. Waste less. Make healthier everyday choices.**
